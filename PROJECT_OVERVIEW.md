# MentorMatch AI — Project Overview

## Problem
Students often struggle to identify alumni who have relevant experience for their exact career goals. Alumni may be willing to help but are difficult to discover and match at scale.

## Solution
MentorMatch creates structured student and alumni profiles, calculates explainable compatibility, recommends mentors, supports mentorship requests, and generates a practical career roadmap.

## End-to-end flow
Student onboarding -> profile analysis -> skill-gap view -> alumni matching -> explainable recommendations -> mentorship request -> roadmap -> progress/feedback.

## Hackathon pitch
"From asking everyone for advice to finding the right person in seconds."

## Future AI upgrades
- Sentence-transformer embeddings for semantic matching
- LLM profile summarization
- Resume/GitHub parsing
- Personalized mentor conversation assistant
- Skill-gap extraction from job descriptions
- Feedback-based re-ranking
